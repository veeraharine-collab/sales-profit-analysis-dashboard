# sales-profit-analysis-dashboard
Excel-based Sales &amp; Profit Analysis Dashboard analyzing sales, profit, quantity, discounts, regional performance, product categories, and yearly/monthly trends.
#Project Overview

This project is an Excel-based Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard designed to analyze business performance and identify important trends across sales, profit, quantity sold, discounts, regions, product categories, and time periods.

The project transforms raw sales data into meaningful summaries and visual insights that can support business decision-making.
# Objectives
Analyze overall sales and profit performance
Track sales trends across different years and months
Compare performance across regions
Analyze sales and profit by product category
Understand quantity sold and discount patterns
Identify high-performing business areas
Create an interactive and easy-to-understand Excel dashboard

# Analysis Performed
1. Yearly Analysis

Sales, quantity, discount, and profit were analyzed across:

2022
2023
2024

This helps identify changes in business performance over time.

2. Monthly Sales Analysis

Monthly sales performance was analyzed to identify sales trends and seasonal patterns throughout the year.

3. Regional Analysis

The dashboard analyzes:

East
North
South

Regional performance is compared using sales, quantity, discount, and profit.

4. Product Category Analysis

The project analyzes major product categories including:

Clothing
Electronics
Furniture
Groceries

Sales, quantity, profit, and discount performance are compared across categories.

# Dashboard

The final dashboard presents the analyzed information in a visual format, allowing users to quickly understand sales and profitability patterns.

It combines KPI cards, charts, and summarized analysis to make the data easier to interpret.

# Key Insights

From the analysis:

2023 recorded the highest yearly sales among the available years.
East region generated the highest sales and profit compared with the other analyzed regions.
Electronics was the strongest-performing category in terms of sales and profit among the displayed categories.
Sales performance varies considerably across months, indicating possible seasonal trends.
The dashboard can help identify areas where discounts and sales performance should be monitored together.
